\# **LOKOJA FLOOD ANALYSIS/PREDICTION SYSTEM  PROJECT**



\## Part 1: The Question`



Which built-up areas of Lokoja are mostly affected by flooding,

and what physical and urban characteristics distinguish these

flood-prone areas from less affected parts of the city?



\## Part 2: Why It Matters



Lokoja's location at the confluence of the Niger and Benue Rivers

makes flood exposure an important urban planning and disaster-risk

management concern. The project will identify recurring flood

hotspots and quantify the buildings, roads and population exposed

to flooding. The results will support urban planning, emergency

response and flood-risk reduction.



\## Part 3: The Data I Need



\- Digital Elevation Model 

\- Sentinel-1 SAR 

\- Sentinel-2 

\- Rainfall/Meteorological data

\- Buildings, roads, and infrastructure data 

\- Population data

\- Historical flood extents 

\- Field GPS observations

\- Administrative boundaries.

\## Part 5: What I Would Build



A GIS-based Lokoja Flood Watch system that maps historical flood

extent, identifies recurring flood hotspots and estimates exposed

buildings, roads and population. The system will provide an

interactive map/dashboard for exploring flood events and their

spatial impacts. The project can later be extended to

automatically process new Sentinel-1 imagery after major rainfall

events.
